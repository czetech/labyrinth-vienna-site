09:00 – 13:00 · Morning Teaching Sessions

13:00 – 15:00 · Shared Lunch & Rest

15:00 – 18:00 · Afternoon Teaching Sessions

18:00 onwards · Dinner, Fire Jam Sessions, Rehearsals, Concerts & Evening Gatherings

Beyond the scheduled sessions, Labyrinth Vienna offers many opportunities for spontaneous music-making, exchange, and community throughout the day.

Sunday

The final day is dedicated to celebration and sharing. Participants have the opportunity to perform in the Student Concerts, followed by performances by the \*Labyrinth Vienna Masters\*. These concerts are open to the public and bring the week to a festive close.
